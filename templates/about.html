About the Project

Speech Recognition and Meeting Summarization Using AI

Project overview

AI Meeting Assistant converts a meeting recording into a written transcript and creates a concise summary with key points, decisions and action items. All AI models are open-source and run locally, so no paid API key is needed and recordings are not sent to a third-party service.

How it works

  1. The audio is validated and converted with FFmpeg to 16 kHz mono WAV.
  2. Long audio is split into chunks at quiet moments.
  3. OpenAI Whisper ({{ whisper_models|join(' / ') }}) transcribes each chunk and the text is combined.
  4. A Hugging Face model ({{ summarizer }}) summarises the transcript chunk by chunk.
  5. Key points, decisions and action items are extracted from sentences that actually appear in the transcript.

Accuracy and limitations

  • The summary is AI-generated and may rephrase or miss details. Always check it against the transcript.
  • Decisions and action items are never invented. Missing owners or deadlines are shown as "Not specified."
  • Speech recognition quality depends on audio clarity, accents and background noise.
  • Extraction rules and the summarisation model work best with English transcripts.
  • Long recordings take time on a CPU. Real-time transcription is not supported.
